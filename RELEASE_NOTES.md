# Release notes — Book My Tech Mechanic

Newest first. Each entry is what testers see in TestFlight ("What to test")
and Google Play (internal testing release notes), so it is written for them:
plain British English, no internal jargon. Play allows 500 characters per
release, so the **Store text** block must stay under that. When the two
stores' previous builds differ, the store text covers everything new since
the OLDER one.

## 1.0.0 — 25 Nov 2026 (iOS build 8 · Android build 8)

Store text:

> New: if a customer asks to move a job, you can accept their time or suggest another. Starting the journey keeps the booked time.
>
> Your location is shared while you're on the way, even when you switch to your sat-nav.
>
> Fixed photo uploads. Documents up to 25 MB. Mileage is now needed to complete any job. Removed "Open in Waze".

(Android testers last had build 3, so this covers everything since then;
iOS testers had build 7, which already included the location, photo,
document and mileage changes.)

## 1.0.0 — 25 Nov 2026 (iOS build 7)

- Location is shared in the background while on the way, with a heartbeat so
  a stationary mechanic doesn't drop off the customer's map.
- Photos are shrunk before upload; documents upload straight to storage (up
  to 10 MB in this build).
- Mileage is required to complete every job, and shown in the sign-off.
- "Open in Waze" removed.
